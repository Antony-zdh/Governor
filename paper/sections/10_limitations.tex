\section*{Limitations}
\label{sec:limitations}

\paragraph{Scope of the negative result.}
The central result---every rule loses and none clears the conservative
gate---is established on $18$ development environments (two models, three
benchmarks, seeds $42/43/44$) and confirmed on the held-out test split and two
unseen models (Section~\ref{sec:results}). It is a statement about the space
searched: one boxed-answer probe suffix (\simplek{}) and one preregistered
schema ($17{,}712$ rules, four families, seven dimensions). Other probe wordings
and rule dimensions outside this schema are not searched; the mechanism
(Section~\ref{sec:mechanism}) is our argument that they would not help, but it is
an argument, not an exhaustive proof.

\paragraph{The exact floor is a development quantity.}
The $1.85\pp$ least-bad-rule value has a bootstrap $95\%$ CI of $[0.0,5.6]\pp$ and
measures $0.11\pp$ on test; we therefore do not rest any claim on it
(Section~\ref{sec:floor}). The load-bearing findings are the distribution-free
ones---all $17{,}712$ rules lose, the empty joint gate---which do not depend on
the floor's point estimate.

\paragraph{Held-out architecture was re-collected.}
The held-out architecture model (Llama-8B) was re-collected after we found and
fixed a chat-template defect (a missing model-specific beginning-of-sequence
token) that had degraded its earlier generations; the corrected model scores
$\sim\!88\%$ on \textsc{MATH}500, and the confirmation uses the corrected
trajectories.

\paragraph{Small held-out sets on hard benchmarks.}
AIME24 and AMC23 are small (dev splits of $6$ and $8$ problems per seed), so a
single problem is a large fraction and per-benchmark cells are noisy. We
aggregate over seeds and treat the per-model gate as primary and the
per-benchmark gate as a diagnostic (Section~\ref{sec:resolution}).

\paragraph{Probe-tax dependence of the savings axis.}
Net-savings numbers charge dense re-probing (every $64$ tokens, $32$-token
probes); a sparser or KV-cache-reusing schedule would change the \emph{savings}
axis and could move some rules to positive net savings. We separate gross from
net savings (Section~\ref{sec:mechanism}) precisely so the probe-tax-dependent
part is distinguishable; the accuracy-tax result does not depend on the schedule.

\paragraph{The constructive signal is exploratory.}
The boundary-confidence controller of Appendix~\ref{sec:boundary} spans three dev
seeds but is seed-sensitive (macro $\Delta$acc per-seed range $[-6.1,+4.2]\pp$;
mean $-0.75\pp$), model-dependent, not part of the preregistered sweep, and has no
test-split confirmation. It should be read as evidence that a non-consensus
signal escapes the accuracy tax, not as a finished method.

\paragraph{Domain.}
All benchmarks are competition mathematics with checkable final answers. Whether
false consensus and the accuracy tax transfer to open-ended reasoning, code, or
agentic tasks is out of scope.
