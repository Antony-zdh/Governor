\section{Comparison to Prior Early-Stoppers}
\label{sec:baselines}

Our rule family is deliberately broad, but a reader will still ask how
\emph{prior-work} early-stoppers behave on the same trajectories. We specify a
common harness---the same frozen trajectories, the same \simplek{} probe bank,
and the same token accounting (probe output tokens included)---so that every
method is compared apples-to-apples on the $18$ dev environments.

\paragraph{Methods.}
\textbf{(i) CertaIndex / Dynasor} \citep{dynasor_certaindex}: a share-threshold
stop over recent probe answers. \textbf{(ii) Entropy threshold}: stop when the
probe-answer distribution's entropy drops below a threshold. \textbf{(iii)
Patience consensus}: stop after $k$ consecutive identical probe answers. Each is
instantiated at the operating point its authors recommend, plus a
conservative/moderate/aggressive sweep.
